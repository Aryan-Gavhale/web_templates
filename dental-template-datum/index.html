07

Notes

The seven things worth reading before you spend anything.

Notes to the drawing.

  1. Note 01 · Bone

    An implant is only as good as what holds it. If the CBCT shows too little bone we will say so at the consultation, and the plan becomes graft first, fixture at four to six months. We would rather lose the case than place into nothing.

  2. Note 02 · Smoking

    Smoking roughly doubles the failure rate and roughly halves the graft success rate. We will still treat you. We will also put the figures in the written plan, because consent without numbers is not consent.

  3. Note 03 · Timescale

    Simple single tooth: fourteen weeks from placement to final crown. With grafting, six to nine months. Anyone promising a finished implant in a fortnight is either describing a provisional or is not describing this procedure.

  4. Note 04 · Pain

    Most people take ibuprofen for two days and go back to work the next morning. Placement itself is less involved than an extraction — there is pressure and noise, and no sensation in the site at all.

  5. Note 05 · Referrals

    We take referrals from thirty-one practices across West Yorkshire and send every patient back to their own dentist for routine care. We do not compete for your check-ups.

  6. Note 06 · Warranty

    Ten years on the fixture, five on the crown, provided you attend the annual review. A failed fixture is replaced at our cost, including the laboratory work. Grafts are excluded, because biology is not a component.

  7. Note 07 · Records

    You get the scan, the plan, the fixture batch numbers and the torque values, as files, at the end of treatment. They belong to you and any dentist you see next will want them.

  8. Note 08 · Second opinions

    Bring the plan you were given elsewhere. We will tell you whether we would do the same thing, and we will not undercut a sound plan simply to win the work. Roughly one consultation in nine ends with us sending someone back to their own dentist.
