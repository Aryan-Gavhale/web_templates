04 — Technology

Equipment we own, not equipment we outsource.

Nothing leaves the building — no external lab, no external radiology, no waiting on a courier.

Imaging

Cone-beam CT, 80 µm voxel

A 14-second scan replaces referral radiology. Bone volume measured before a fixture is chosen.

Chairside

Intraoral optical scanning

No trays, no alginate. A 90-second sweep produces a model accurate to 20 microns.

In-house lab

Five-axis ceramic milling

Crowns designed, milled, glazed and seated between morning and afternoon.

Surgery

Er:YAG laser & piezo surgery

Selective cutting of hard tissue with the nerve bundle left intact. Less swelling, fewer sutures.

Comfort

Monitored conscious sedation

An anaesthetist on rota, full vitals monitoring, and a recovery bay with its own nurse.
