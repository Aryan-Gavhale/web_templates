02 — Position

We do not decorate rooms. We rebuild how they behave.

Most interiors fail quietly — a socket in the wrong place, a door that swings into the light, a ceiling that flattens by four o'clock. We survey, model and re-plan before a single finish is chosen.

Everything is drawn in-house and built by fabricators we have used for a decade. One team from first survey to final snag, so nothing is lost in translation.

05 — Anatomy of a Room

Tap a marker

Every decision below was made before the walls were painted. Explore the layers of a finished UMBRA interior.

01 — Ceiling

Cloud pendant, hung at 2140mm

Low enough to make the room feel held, high enough to walk under. The fitting is dimmed on its own circuit so it can drop to candle level after nine.

02 — Wall

Lime plaster, five coats, burnished

Applied by hand and polished with stone. It absorbs the mid-tones and stops the corners from reading as hard lines.

03 — Seating

Bouclé modular, 620mm seat depth

Deep enough to fold your legs onto, shallow enough to eat from. Removable covers, because a sofa should survive a decade of actual life.

04 — Floor

Hand-knotted wool, undyed

Sized to sit under the front legs of every seat so the arrangement reads as one object rather than furniture placed near a rug.

05 — Aperture

Full-height glazing, no head detail

The frame is buried in the reveal so the opening reads as a hole in the wall. Linen sheers diffuse the west light without dimming it.

08 — Questions

Before you write

  • Yes — roughly a third of our work is in mainland Europe, with completed projects in Lisbon, Copenhagen, Antwerp and Rotterdam. We travel for survey, mid-build and snagging, and work with a local contractor for everything in between.
