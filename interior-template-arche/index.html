(01) — The Studio

We begin with the room as it is: its light, its proportion, its history. Nothing is added until it earns its place. What remains is an interior that feels inevitable — warm, exact, and made to age beautifully.

a.

Material honesty

Lime plaster, unlacquered brass, quarried travertine and solid oak — surfaces that record time rather than resist it.

b.

Drawn to the millimetre

Every reveal, shadow gap and joinery detail is documented in-house and built with makers we have worked with for a decade.

(03) — Process

Four movements,
one room.

01 / 04
  1. 01

    Listening

    We spend a full day in the space with you — how you wake, where you read, which wall the afternoon sun claims. The brief is written afterwards, never before.

    • Site survey
    • Lifestyle study
    • Light mapping
  2. 02

    Composition

    Plans, sections and hand elevations. We model the room in physical card at 1:20 before a single render exists, because proportion is felt, not previewed.

    • Spatial planning
    • 1:20 models
    • Material board
  3. 03

    Making

    Joinery drawings go directly to our workshops in Brianza and Jutland. We visit the bench, approve the grain, and sign off each finish in daylight.

    • Bespoke joinery
    • Site direction
    • Finish approval
  4. 04

    Settling

    We style, photograph and then leave. Twelve months later we return to see how the space has worn — and adjust what the years have taught us.

    • Styling
    • Handover book
    • One-year review
