The studio

A room is a machine for behaviour. Ours are built to make people stay twenty minutes longer than they meant to.

We document properly

Every project leaves here as a full set — plans, RCPs, elevations, joinery details, finishes schedule. Builders price it accurately because there is nothing left to guess at.

We stay on site

One of the three of us is at every site meeting from demolition to defects. Not a graduate with a camera — the person who drew it.

We buy well

Fourteen years of trade accounts, local makers and a stubborn preference for things that can be repaired. It shows up in the second year, not the first.
