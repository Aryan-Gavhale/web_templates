Questions

Asked most often.

Anything not here, ring the studio and ask — 0114 496 0180. The desk can answer most of it without putting you through to a clinician.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, private only. There is no registration fee and no waiting list — the next check-up slot is usually within a week, and emergency slots are held back daily.

Is the price really the price?

Yes. You get a written quote before anything starts, and the invoice matches it. If we find something mid-treatment we stop, tell you, and re-quote.

I am genuinely frightened of dentists.

Say so when you book and it goes at the top of your notes. You can come and sit in the room with nothing happening as many times as you like. Sedation is available for anything long.

Can I pay in instalments?

Interest free over six or twelve months on anything above £300, arranged at the desk in ten minutes. Longer terms are available through a finance provider.

Do you see children?

From two years old. Free on the Family plan until they are eighteen, and £25 a visit otherwise. The first appointment is a ride in the chair and a look.

What if I need something you do not do?

We will say so and refer you, with the notes and scans sent ahead. You will not be charged for the appointment that worked that out.
