Who we are

We built a hospital, not a clinic — then made it feel like neither.

Northlight began in 1994 as two surgeries above a chemist on Halden Street. Thirty years later it occupies the whole building: four floors, twenty-four chairs, its own imaging suite and laboratory, and a north-facing window wall that gives every room the same even light a studio gets.

That is not decoration. Shade-matching a crown under daylight rather than a lamp is the difference between a tooth that disappears and one you notice in photographs for the next fifteen years.

Your first visit

Ninety minutes, and no treatment.

Nothing is done to your teeth on the first day unless you are in pain. The first appointment exists to produce a plan you understand and agree to.

  1. 01

    Twenty minutes talking

    History, worries, what you want your teeth to do for you. No chair yet.

  2. 02

    Examination and imaging

    Charting, photographs and the X-rays your case needs, read with you on the screen.

  3. 03

    Options, in order of cost

    Including the option of doing nothing yet, which is sometimes the right one.

  4. 04

    A written estimate

    Itemised, valid ninety days, and emailed to you before you reach the front door.

Questions

Answered plainly.

Do I need a referral?

No. Every speciality here takes patients directly, including implantology and oral surgery. If you do have a referral, bring it and we will keep the referring dentist informed.

Will the estimate change?

Only if something changes clinically, and then we stop and re-quote before continuing. Last year 99.4% of estimates were billed exactly as written.

Can I pay over time?

Yes, interest-free over six or twelve months on treatment above £500, arranged at reception in about ten minutes. We do not take a deposit to hold an appointment.

What about anxiety?

Tell us when you ring. You can visit the building, sit in the room and leave again without treatment, as many times as you need. Sedation is available for anything longer than an hour.

Are you taking NHS patients?

Children under eighteen, yes. Adult lists are private, though the emergency service is open to anyone who rings, registered here or not.

Can I see my records?

They are yours. X-rays, photographs, notes and estimates are sent to you or to another dentist on request, at no charge, usually within two working days.
