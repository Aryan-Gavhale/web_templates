A printed surgical guide from your own scan, so the fixture goes where it was planned rather than where the drill wanders. Single units through to full-arch rehabilitation.

  • Ten-year fixture warranty with kept recalls
  • Sedation available for longer sittings

From $1,850 per fixture

Clear aligner therapy

A simulated trajectory before you commit, showing exactly which teeth move, in what order, and what the twenty-sixth week looks like. Refinements included, not billed.

  • Monthly review, no charge per visit

Five stages. You always know which one you are in.

Each stage ends with something in writing — a chart, an estimate, a scan or a discharge note — so nothing about your treatment lives only in a conversation.

    Assessment

    Sixty minutes. Full charting, photographs and a scan. No treatment on the day.

  4. 04
