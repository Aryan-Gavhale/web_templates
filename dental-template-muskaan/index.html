Common questions

Asked at the front desk, every day.

Will the price change after the X-ray?

The starting prices on this page are honest starting prices. After the X-ray you get one written estimate, you sign it, and that is the number on your bill. If we find something extra mid-treatment we stop and re-quote first.

Is the EMI really at zero interest?

Yes, on treatment above ₹5,000, for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months. We pay the finance charge, not you. You need a PAN card and a debit or credit card, and approval takes about ten minutes at the counter.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are seen at every centre, though you may wait 30 to 45 minutes at peak hours. Pain and swelling are always seen the same day, appointment or not.

Which languages do you speak?

Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada across the group, and at least three at every centre. Tell us on the call and we will put you with a dentist you can talk to comfortably.

Can I get my records if I move city?

Your X-rays, photographs and treatment notes are on one file across all eight centres, and we will email the whole thing to you or to another dentist on request, free.

Still not sure?

Send us a photograph on WhatsApp. A dentist, not a bot, will tell you whether you need to come in at all.
