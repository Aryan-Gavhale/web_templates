    06

    Notices

    The six things worth reading before you travel.

    • In pain today

      Ring at 08.00. We hold two chairs a day for pain and we do not ask you to prove it. If we cannot see you, we will tell you who can, that morning, not next week.

    • Running late

      Ring us. Up to ten minutes and we will usually still see you. Beyond that we will rebook rather than rush the appointment or push everyone behind you back.

    • Missed appointments

      Twenty-four hours' notice and there is no charge. Less than that and we charge half the fee, because the chair sat empty and someone in pain wanted it.

    • Step-free access

      Level entrance from Cambridge Street, a lift to all three surgeries, and a hearing loop at reception. Tell us in advance and we will meet you at the door.

    • Children

      Free on the NHS with us until eighteen. First visit is a look, a count and a ride in the chair — nothing else, however much you would like it to be.

    • Nervous patients

      Say so when you book and we give you a longer slot, a first appointment where nothing is done, and a hand signal that stops everything. It gets used often.
