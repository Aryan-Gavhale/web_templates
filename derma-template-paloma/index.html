Uno · Tu fototipo

Skin doesn't come
in one setting.

Almost everything in dermatology — laser choice, pigment risk, how a scar heals, how often you should be screened — turns on your Fitzpatrick phototype. Pick yours. The advice changes.

I

Phototype I

Always burns, never tans

The highest skin-cancer risk of the six, and the lowest risk of pigment trouble after a procedure.

Sun
SPF 50 daily, reapplied. Burns in under 20 minutes at this altitude.
Lasers
Nearly the full bench is available, including ablative resurfacing.
Pigment
Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is uncommon. Redness lingers instead.
Screening
Full-body mole check every 12 months from age 25.

Phototype is a starting point, not a diagnosis. It is confirmed in person — two people who tick the same box can still need different settings.

Tres · Cómo trabajamos

Four steps.
No upsell in any of them.

The consult costs what it costs whether you buy anything afterwards or not. Nothing is sold from the treatment chair.

  1. 1

    Escucha

    Forty minutes, most of it you talking. What you have tried, what it cost, what went wrong, what you actually want to stop worrying about.

  2. 2

    Mira

    Full-skin examination under fixed light, dermoscopy, and photographs in the same position every time so a change is a change and not a different angle.

  3. 3

    Plan

    One page, in plain Spanish or English: what we are treating, with what, for how long, at what total cost, and the date we decide whether it worked.

  4. 4

    Revisa

    The review is booked before you leave and included in the price. A plan that has not worked by then gets changed, not repeated.

Seis · Preguntas

Asked before
every first visit.

Materially, yes. A 755 nm alexandrite that is routine on phototype II can cause lasting pigment loss on phototype V. Higher phototypes carry a much greater risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation and keloid, so settings go down and intervals go up. It also changes screening: melanoma in darker skin turns up on palms, soles and nail beds, which is where most examinations never look.

$1,200 MXN for forty minutes: history, full-skin examination, dermoscopy, photographs and a written one-page plan with the total cost of anything proposed. Nothing is charged on top of that on the day, and nothing is sold in the room.

The practice invoices directly and issues a factura you can claim against GNP, AXA, Metlife and Bupa. Medical dermatology is usually reimbursed; aesthetic work is not, and we tell you which side of the line you are on before you agree to anything.

Two slots are held at 08:00 every weekday and never released to routine booking. Telephone rather than using the form. A lesion that is bleeding, growing or newly pigmented is examined that day and excised at the same visit where it is indicated.

Frequently. About one first visit in five ends with a sunscreen recommendation and no plan at all, and the consult fee still covers it. A practice that could not afford to say no would be a bad place to ask.
