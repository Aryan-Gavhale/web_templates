Approach

A SHOP IS NOT A CONTAINER FOR STOCK

It is a sequence of decisions about where a person's eyes go next, and in what order. Get the order right and the room does the selling. Get it wrong and no amount of material budget will rescue it.

We start every project on the pavement, not the plan. What is visible from six metres away, from three, from the threshold. What the hand reaches for first. Where the room gives someone permission to slow down.

Then we draw it — properly, at 1:20, with the fixtures dimensioned and the lighting scheduled — because a retail interior is a manufactured object and it will be built by people who were not in the room when it was designed.

Vitrine is fourteen people on the third floor of a former shipping office on Kloosterstraat. Architects, a graphic designer, a lighting specialist and two people whose entire job is making sure what we drew is what gets built.

We work for independents and for groups, and we price both the same way. If a project is right for us we will say so in a week; if it is not, we will say that too, and tell you who to call instead.
