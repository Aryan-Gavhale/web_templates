Our position

The best cosmetic work is the work nobody notices.

We do not whiten to a shade that does not exist in nature, and we do not remove sound enamel to make a case easier to fit. Most of what we do is subtractive in the smallest possible sense — and reversible for as long as it can be.

  • Minimal preparation

    Between 0.3 and 0.5 mm of reduction. Where no reduction is needed, none is made.

  • One pair of hands

    The clinician who designs the case cuts it, seats it and reviews it. No handover.

  • Rehearsed first

    Every aesthetic case is worn as a trial smile before a single tooth is touched.

The visit

Nothing irreversible happens early.

  1. First visit

    Photographs, scan, conversation

    Ninety minutes. We take records and listen. No treatment is carried out and nothing is sold on the day.

  2. Ten days later

    The plan, in writing

    Staged, itemised and fixed for six months. It arrives by post as well as by email, because people read paper more carefully.

  3. Before consent

    The rehearsal

    A trial smile, worn for a week in your own life. Photograph it, show it to people who will tell you the truth, then come back and change it.

  4. Treatment

    Long appointments, few of them

    We would rather see you three times for three hours than nine times for one. Mornings are kept for the longest cases.

  5. Afterwards

    Reviewed, not released

    At one week, three months and then annually. The guarantee holds as long as you keep coming, and the records are kept indefinitely.

Investment

Published, and explained.

These are starting figures, not quotations. Your written plan gives one number per stage, fixed for six months, with the laboratory fee shown separately.

Ninety minutes with photographs, an intraoral scan and a written plan posted to you within ten days. Deducted in full from the first stage of treatment if you proceed within six months.

Hand-layered feldspathic porcelain, including the trial smile, one remake of that trial smile at no charge, and the bonded placement. Laboratory fee is itemised at roughly a third of the figure.

Cone-beam scan, printed surgical guide, fixture, healing period, custom abutment and the final crown. Bone grafting, where the anatomy needs it, is quoted separately before consent.

Carried out under the microscope, usually in one visit, with a post-operative scan filed to your record. Retreatment of work done elsewhere is quoted after assessment.

Too variable to publish honestly. Cases have ranged from £14,000 to £60,000 over six to nine months, and the plan always separates what is necessary from what is elective.

Payment. Plans above £3,000 can be staged over twelve months at no interest. We are not registered with any insurer and do not accept third-party payment, which is why the fee list can stay this short.

Do you take emergencies?
For registered patients, the same day, always. If you are not registered we will see you if we can, and tell you honestly if someone else can see you sooner.
Can I bring a plan from elsewhere?
Please do. Second opinions are a third of our consultations, and we are perfectly willing to tell you that the plan you have is a good one.
Is sedation available?
Yes, with a consultant anaesthetist for longer appointments. Most patients find the long-appointment format means they need it less than they expected.
How long does the guarantee last?
Ten years on ceramics and implants, in writing, conditional only on attending your annual review. It transfers if you move away and we help you find someone.
