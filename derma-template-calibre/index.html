01

Measure first.
Diagnose second.

The first appointment is ninety minutes of instrumentation. Nothing is prescribed in it. You leave with a printed baseline and a date.

Baseline readout · anonymised specimen

  • Melanin index 0
  • Erythema index 0
  • Surface texture 0
  • Pore volume 0
  • Barrier loss (TEWL) 0
  • UV damage, cumulative 0

Indices are normalised 0–100 against a Fitzpatrick-matched reference set. The same six are repeated at every review, on the same device, in the same room, at the same time of day.

Imaging

Cross-polarised and UV capture at fixed distance, plus reflectance confocal microscopy where a lesion needs cellular resolution rather than a second opinion.

06

Before
admission.

Anything not answered here, the practice manager takes calls in person between 08:00 and 17:00 CET.

Because a measurement taken after a treatment has begun is worthless as a baseline. Ninety minutes buys a full-body dermoscopic survey, a calibrated capture and six indices — the reference every later visit is compared against. Treating on day zero would destroy it.

No referral is needed. Medically indicated work is billed under Swiss TARMED and is generally reimbursable through basic or supplementary insurance; aesthetic work is not, and is quoted privately. You are told which category you fall into before any money changes hands.

The plan is closed. Every written plan states a target and an expiry date; if the index has not reached the target by the review, the course is not extended and remaining sessions are refunded. Roughly one plan in nine ends this way, and that figure is published each January.

Dr Naumann, on every case, without exception. The practice employs two nurses for phlebotomy, dressings and aftercare. No device on the bench is operated by anyone else, which is the reason the daily list is capped at nine.

On an encrypted server in Zürich, under Swiss FADP, retained ten years and never used in publication or marketing without separate written consent that you may withdraw at any point. Withdrawing it has no effect on your care.

Inside forty-eight hours. Two slots are held every weekday at 07:30 and are never released to routine booking. A lesion that is bleeding, changing or newly pigmented is excised at that visit where indicated, with histology on the specimen.
