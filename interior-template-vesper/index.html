The studio

Eleven people,
one long table

Vesper was founded in 2004 above a shuttered bar on Via Palermo. We still work there. Eleven of us — architects, a lighting designer, a joiner who keeps everyone honest — and one table long enough that nobody has their own end of it.

We take four projects a year. Every one of them gets a partner on site until the last chair is placed, because the difference between a good room and a great one is decided in the final fortnight, not the first.
