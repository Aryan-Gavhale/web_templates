A-01 Scope of works

Drawings first. Always has been.

01

Survey & feasibility

We measure the room ourselves, in person, with a laser and a tape as a check. Half the flats in this city are not the shape their deed says they are, and finding that out in week one is much cheaper than finding it out in week twenty.

02

Design & documentation

Plans, RCPs, elevations, joinery sections at 1:5, a finishes schedule and a lighting layout with every circuit named. The set is issued as one PDF with a revision history, so nobody is building from a drawing that was superseded in April.

03

Tender & site

We price the work with three contractors we trust, sit in on the negotiation, and then attend site fortnightly until handover. Snagging is done against the drawings, not against a feeling.

A-04 Method

The set, in the order it is issued

  1. A-01

    Measured survey

    Laser survey, photographs, a note of every service and every lie the floor plan tells.

    2 weeks
  2. A-02

    Concept plan

    Two or three plan options at 1:50, drawn far enough to be costed and argued about.

    4 weeks
  3. A-03

    Developed design

    One plan taken forward. Elevations, RCP, a first finishes schedule and a budget you can trust.

    6 weeks
  4. A-04

    Construction set

    Everything at 1:20, joinery at 1:5, every circuit named and every junction resolved.

    8 weeks
  5. A-05

    Tender

    Three contractors, one comparable price, one negotiation we sit in on.

    4 weeks
  6. A-06

    Site & handover

    Fortnightly visits, revision drawings as needed, snagging against the set rather than against a mood.

    Duration

A-05 Enquiry

Send us a plan and a date.

Even a rough sketch on squared paper is enough to start. Tell us the address, roughly what you want to spend and when you would like to be living in it. We answer every enquiry within five working days, including the ones we turn down.
