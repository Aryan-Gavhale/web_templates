Our approach

We start with how you actually live, not a mood board.

Beautiful rooms are easy to photograph and hard to live in. We spend the first fortnight learning your routines, then design backwards from them.

  1. 01

    A fortnight of listening

    Two visits, a long questionnaire, and a look inside your cupboards. It tells us more than any brief.

  2. 02

    One scheme, fully drawn

    No three-option guessing games. We present a single considered scheme and refine it with you.

  3. 03

    We stay until it's right

    Sourcing, deliveries, trades and the final styling day. You are not left with a folder of links.

Room by room

Every room has a different job

The room that has to do everything

Reading, television, homework, guests, and a dog that sheds. We plan for all five rather than styling for none of them — layered lighting, a sofa you can actually lie on, and surfaces that forgive.

  • Typical spend£14k–£30k
  • Timeline8–12 weeks
  • IncludesLayout, lighting, joinery, styling

Where the house actually gathers

We design kitchens around the twenty minutes before dinner, when three people need the same square metre. That means real circulation, honest worktops, and storage sized to what you own.

  • Typical spend£28k–£60k
  • Timeline14–20 weeks
  • IncludesCabinetry, appliances, stone, lighting

A room that gets darker on purpose

Bedrooms are the one place we design for the eyes closing, not opening. Warm low circuits, blackout built into the joinery, and nothing shiny in the line of sight from the pillow.

  • Typical spend£9k–£18k
  • Timeline6–9 weeks
  • IncludesWardrobes, lighting, textiles, paint

Small, wet, and unforgiving

The room where bad detailing shows up fastest. We draw every tile course and junction before anything is ordered, and specify for steam, splash and a decade of scrubbing.

  • Typical spend£11k–£24k
  • Timeline8–14 weeks
  • IncludesLayout, tiling, brassware, ventilation

How it goes

Four stages, no surprises

  1. 01

    Call and visit

    A free half-hour call, then a paid survey visit where we measure, photograph and ask a lot of questions. You get a written scope and a fixed fee before anything else happens.

    Week 1–2 · Free, then £450
  2. 02

    Design and drawings

    Layouts, elevations, lighting plans and a full material palette you can hold. One scheme, presented properly, then two rounds of refinement included as standard.

    Week 3–7 · 40% of fee
  3. 03

    Sourcing and build

    We place the orders, chase the lead times and run the trades. You get a weekly note on Fridays telling you exactly what moved and what did not.

    Week 8–18 · 40% of fee
  4. 04

    Styling and handover

    Two days on site placing everything, a snag list we own rather than you, and a care guide for every finish in the house. Then we leave you alone.

    Week 19–20 · 20% of fee
